# U1, first round: the frame — plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-u1-frame-and-full-screen-design.md` §1–§6. Web only (`apps/web`).

Each task ends with its tests green (only the files it touches, one process at a time) and a commit.

1. **The frame and the full screen.** `index.css`: `--container-frame`, `--container-frame-full`,
   `--container-measure` in `@theme`. `components/layout/frame.ts`: `PageWidth`, `PAGE_FRAME` (the margins, `box-content`,
   centred), `FULL_PAGE_FRAME`. `Layout`: the content area takes `PAGE_FRAME` with `max-w-frame`, widened to
   `max-w-frame-full` in full screen or on a `full` page (`:has()`), the vertical padding unchanged, no `max-w-4xl py-12`;
   the button named «Tutto schermo» with `aria-pressed`, `aria-keyshortcuts` and its `title`, still under reduced motion.
   `PageFrame`: `width` (`reading` wraps the body at the measure; every width written on the root),
   `FOCUS_BUTTON_CLEARANCE` against `100%`. `KeyboardShortcutsModal`: «Tutto schermo», and its two English entries in
   Italian. `Scrivania`: one height on a phone, Focus mode's 3 rem gone. Tests: `theme.test.ts` (the tokens, the `:has`
   class, the clearance), the Layout tests, `PageFrame.test.tsx`, `Scrivania.phone.test.tsx`.
2. **One limit per page.** `SearchPage` declares `full` and loses `ReaderLayout` (deleted); `PracticeTable` and the
   loading skeleton lose `max-w-4xl`; `ReadingRow` stays in its parent (no overshoot); `RassegneSearchPage` and
   `ConnectAiAppPage` declare `reading`; «Crediti» takes `PAGE_FRAME`. Tests: `ReadingRow.test.tsx` (the row stays in the
   frame), `PageFrame.test.tsx` (the section pages, the reviews among them, write no cap of their own), a test of the
   search page's width.
3. **Gymnasium's «Oggi».** `ReviewHome` splits from `xl` in full screen too. `ReviewHome.test.tsx`.
4. **The reading measure.** Every `max-w-[NNch]` → `max-w-measure` (one short line → `max-w-prose`); `.vlx-art` reads the
   token; the signs' container query at `70ch`. Tests: `theme.test.ts` (the token compiles, `.vlx-art` reads it, the
   query's number is the token's), a guard on `[NNch]` in the sources, `ReviewSession.test.tsx`.
5. **The docs.** `apps/web/CLAUDE.md`: the frame, the three widths, the full screen, the measure, «Oggi»'s split,
   `ReadingRow`, the reading surface's measure.
6. **After the independent review.** `Layout` says where the full-screen button floats over the page
   (`--focus-button-room`: from `lg`, from `md` in full screen, never over the search page's field) and
   `FOCUS_BUTTON_CLEARANCE` reads it with no breakpoint of its own; the practice's table, alone in its row, keeps the
   same room (in full screen its header and the act's pinned heading were under the button). `cn` knows the theme's
   frame and measure as max-widths. The shortcut's key «Spazio»; stale comments on the padding and `--sidebar-width`.
